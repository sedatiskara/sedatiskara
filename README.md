## Main focus: ReguShield AI

[ReguShield AI](https://regushield.ai) is the project I spend most of my time building.

I am developing compliance intelligence infrastructure for regulated digital businesses, with an initial focus on European requirements and the needs of FinTech, crypto and AI companies.

The goal is to connect regulatory requirements with operational data, controls, evidence and accountable decisions in one workspace.

My development work focuses on:

* Connecting obligations across regulatory frameworks.
* Mapping requirements to controls, evidence and responsible people.
* Tracking regulatory changes and supporting impact assessments.
* Building explainable decisions, review workflows and audit trails.
* Giving teams and leadership a clearer view of compliance gaps and next steps.

Frameworks in focus include MiCA, DORA, the EU AI Act and the EU AML framework.
